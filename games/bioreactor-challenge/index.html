Bioreactor Challenge

Run a 5-litre stirred tank growing medicinal plant cells.
Keep them happy for three weeks, trigger production at the right moment, and harvest the most bioactive compound you can.

Best played full-screen, in landscape. Sound on for your mentor, Aira.
HOW TO PLAY

Grow healthy cells, then harvest the most medicine.

1 · THE GOAL

Maximise YIELD

Harvest as much of the bioactive compound as you can (mg/L), from healthy cells. Day 0 to 21 passes in about 3 minutes.

Score: yield 45 · cell health 15 · process control 20 · setup 10 · elicitor timing 10

2 · READ THE GRAPHS

Each graph moves as time passes. The light-green band is the safe range. Keep pH, oxygen, sugar and temperature inside it. The line turns red when it leaves.

3 · USE THE CONTROLS

Sliders: agitation, aeration and temperature. Plant cells are fragile: slow and gentle. Keep each handle in its green zone.

Buttons: acid / base for pH, feed sucrose, antifoam if foam rises.

Elicitor: one dose around day 12, when the culture is dense. It switches cells from growing to making the compound. Then harvest.
